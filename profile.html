Company Overview

Pioneering Packaging Reliability for Nearly Four Decades

Established in 1986, Suresh Timber Company has grown into one of Western India's foremost manufacturers and suppliers of engineered wooden packaging materials.

From custom-sized pallets, heavy machinery boxes, and open-slat crates in hardwoods, softwoods, or seamless industrial plywood, we provide both air-dried and kiln-seasoned solutions. Operating out of our expansive 67,000 sq ft facility in Fertilizer Nagar, Vadodara, our experienced technicians and modern high-speed sawmills are backed by our own dedicated delivery fleet to ensure dependable, on-schedule logistics across India.

International Standards

Export Compliant ISPM-15 Treatment

International Standards for Phytosanitary Measures No. 15 (ISPM-15) governs wood packaging in international trade to eliminate quarantine pest migrations.

Customs authorities in over 180 countries strictly mandate official treatment stamps. At Suresh Timber Company, every export-grade pallet, crate, and wooden box is processed within our certified facilities and carries an authenticated treatment certificate.

IN - GJ - 042 / HT

Recognized by customs worldwide for seamless port clearance.

Heat Treatment (HT) CODE: HT

Our computerized drying kilns elevate the timber core temperature to a minimum of 56°C for at least 30 consecutive minutes. Completely chemical-free and eco-friendly.
